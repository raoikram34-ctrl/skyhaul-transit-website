Continuous Highway Speed. Priority Dispatch.

Expedited Freight is designed for cargo that simply cannot wait for standard transit schedules. Whether you are running a production plant facing a shutdown, replacing dropped capacity, or fulfilling seasonal high-demand agreements, our expedited service ensures your cargo is in constant motion.

We assign dual-driver teams to your truck. While one driver rests, the other drives, keeping your shipment moving 22 hours a day. Bypassing commercial sorting stops ensures your cargo travels along the most direct geographic routes.

How frequent are GPS tracking updates on expedited loads?

We provide active tracking intervals (every 15 to 30 minutes) on expedited shipments. Shippers receive automated status alerts directly via email or SMS on key location checkpoints.

Are team drivers legally compliant with Hours of Service (HOS)?

Yes. By having one driver sleep in the sleeper berth while the other operates the vehicle, team drivers remain fully compliant with FMCSA HOS rules. The truck stays in motion safely without violating safety regulations.

Do you offer guaranteed delivery times for expedited freight?

Yes, we establish guaranteed delivery window agreements on expedited shipments. If weather or road events occur, our dispatchers proactively re-route teams to meet the required delivery appointments.
