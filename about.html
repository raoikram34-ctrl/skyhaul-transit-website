Carrier Safety

Our Rigorous Safety Vetting Process

We protect your cargo by enforcing the industry's most thorough vetting procedures. Unlike brokerages that simply auto-book loads, we manually verify every carrier's credentials.

Before any trailer approaches your dock, our compliance team validates:

Active FMCSA operating authority (MC/DOT check)
A minimum of $1,000,000 Auto Liability and $100,000 Cargo Liability insurance policies
Favorable safety records and SMS ratings (no alert notifications)
Clean operating history with zero active cargo claims
