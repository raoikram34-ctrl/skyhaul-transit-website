What is transloading, and how does it differ from through-trailer shipping?

Transloading is transferring cargo from one tractor-trailer to another at a border warehouse. Through-trailer shipping keeps the cargo in the same trailer, with only the tractor unit switching at the border. Through-trailer reduces cargo handling but requires trailer pool agreements.

Do I need to hire my own customs broker?

Yes, shippers typically assign their own licensed customs broker to handle commercial duties and custom entry filing. However, Skyhaul Transit coordinates directly with your designated broker to ensure manifest numbers (PAPS/PARS/BARS) align before border arrival.

What safety certifications do cross-border carriers require?

We prioritize carriers certified under safety security programs like **C-TPAT** (Customs-Trade Partnership Against Terrorism), **FAST** (Free and Secure Trade), and **PIP** (Partners in Protection) to bypass dense border inspection lanes and prevent cargo delays.
