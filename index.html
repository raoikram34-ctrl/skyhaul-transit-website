Frequently Answered Questions

Have a Question?

Submit your query directly to our dispatch desk and receive a response in under 15 minutes.

What regions of North America does Skyhaul Transit serve?

We serve customers across the entire United States, Canada, and selected industrial regions of Mexico. Our carrier network supports cross-border shipping, domestic linehauls, and local freight distribution.

How do you verify the safety and compliance of your carriers?

Our compliance team manually verifies active FMCSA authority, safety ratings (SMS scores), and cargo liability insurance policies ($1,000,000 auto liability and $100,000 cargo limits minimum) before booking any carrier.

Can I request a spot quote for a single shipment?

Yes! We handle both contract logistics for high-volume accounts and individual spot-market quotes. You can submit your shipment details via our quote form and receive options in under 15 minutes.

What happens if a driver cancels my load?

We have a dedicated emergency recovery team. When a dropped load or cancellation occurs, we immediately source and dispatch a replacement carrier in your lanes in under 60 minutes to prevent retail chargebacks and line downs.
