Why Choose Full Truckload (FTL)?

Full Truckload (FTL) is the optimal solution when your shipment requires the full space of a 53-foot trailer, exceeds 15,000 pounds, or contains time-sensitive or high-value materials.

By dedicating an entire truck to your cargo, Skyhaul Transit ensures your goods travel directly from your origin dock to your destination. With zero cross-docking or terminal sorting, FTL drastically cuts down transit times and reduces potential damage.

No sorting stops - trailer stays sealed to delivery
Ideal for high-volume, heavy weight, or fragile cargo
Flexible scheduling tailored to your warehouse operation

What is the maximum weight load for FTL transport?

Standard 53-foot dry van and refrigerated trailers can legally transport up to 45,000 lbs of cargo in the United States and Canada (subject to tractor weight limits and state/provincial laws). Specialized trailers may handle higher limits with permits.

How far in advance should I book an FTL load?

For standard dry van routes, booking 24 to 48 hours in advance secures optimal contract pricing. However, we maintain active spot-capacity networks to dispatch trucks on same-day or emergency recovery assignments.

Can I seal the trailer during FTL transport?

Yes, all dedicated FTL shipments can be sealed at your loading dock. The driver will record the high-security seal number on the Bill of Lading (BOL), and the seal remains untouched until arrival to verify cargo integrity.
